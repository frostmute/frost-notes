Operating an apparel brand requires closing the loop between digital design and physical artifact. Since 2020, DeepspaceGhost has served as a vehicle for visual design, typography, and illustration, materialized onto physical garments.

The work is not just the artwork — it is the entire system of delivery. From the Shopify storefront to point-of-sale systems, inventory management, and fulfillment, the project is a holistic exercise in building a self-sustaining retail operation. 

The physical products carry the same structural constraints as my previous production work, but here, the direction is entirely self-determined. You learn very quickly that a compelling graphic is worthless if the screenprinter's halftones blow out, or if the inventory pipeline breaks. The brand is the system that delivers the shirt, not just the shirt itself.
