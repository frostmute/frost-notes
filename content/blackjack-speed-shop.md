Translating the physical reality of an automotive performance shop into a cohesive digital and print identity requires treating the brand itself as an instrument. The work for Black Jack Speed Shop spanned multiple mediums—from the e-commerce web architecture to physical signage and product mock-ups—each demanding a specific approach to precision. 

When you design for an environment where tolerances are measured in thousandths of an inch, the visual identity must carry that same weight. A digital layout or a printed sign cannot just look fast; it must look engineered.

## Digital Showroom

The web design focused on bringing the tactile nature of custom automotive work to the browser, organizing complex service offerings and inventory into a structured, easily navigable hierarchy.

![Web Design Showcase](assets/blackjack-web.png)

## Physical Production

Physical collateral—signage, print materials, and product mockups—served as the bridge between the digital brand and the physical shop floor. Like the challenge coins and insignia work, print demands a different kind of correctness: once it goes to press, there are no hotfixes.
