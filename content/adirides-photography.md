Between 2004 and 2010, I handled product photography and visual design for Accessible Designs Inc. (ADIRides), an online retail and corporate operation. The work involved lighting, capturing, and color-correcting mobility products for e-commerce and advertising. 

It was an early lesson in how images do load-bearing work in a retail environment. The photograph is not just documentation; it is the interface through which the user interacts with the product before it physically arrives. 

If the color correction is off, the customer returns the product. If the lighting doesn't highlight the specific knurling on a wheelchair rim, the user cannot verify it meets their accessibility needs. The camera is just a tool to extract necessary data and package it for someone making a functional decision.
