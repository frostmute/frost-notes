Letters are structural objects before they are semantic ones. This ongoing freelance exploration focuses on abstract letterform, graffiti, calligraphy, and hand-drawn typographic systems.

When you push a character far enough into abstraction, it stops being read and starts being seen as pure geometry. This work lives in that threshold between legibility and iconographic illustration. 

You learn to respect the underlying skeleton of a letter. If you bend the spine too far, the 'A' becomes a triangle. The constraint is retaining the ghost of the character while aggressively modifying the skin.
