This is the oldest thing on the instrument, and it is kept deliberately.

In October 2023 I put up a portfolio and wrote the obligatory first post: who I am, what I do, what I intended to publish. Designer, self-taught developer, San Antonio, two decades of production work behind me. The stated plan was to write about the projects as they happened rather than presenting them afterward as finished objects.

## What the plan got right

Publishing in progress rather than in retrospect. Almost everything on this dial exists because I wrote about a problem while I was still inside it, which forces a kind of honesty that a case study written six months later cannot reach. A retrospective explains why the decisions were correct. A note written mid-build admits which ones you are not sure about.

## What it got wrong

I thought the hard part was going to be having something worth saying. The hard part was the container.

That first site was hosted on someone else's publishing platform. The one after it was a different platform. Then a static generator, then a rebuild, and each move cost me content — not because the text was gone, but because the text was entangled with a format that did not travel. Posts became stubs. Stubs became links to a previous host. Some things did not survive at all.

Which is, in retrospect, the entire thesis of the software I now spend my time building. Every importer, every Markdown bridge, every plain-text stubbornness in the entries under **MECHANE** is a direct descendant of losing my own writing to a platform migration.

I did not know that in 2023. I was just annoyed.

## Why it stays

An archive that only contains work you are still proud of is a portfolio. An archive that contains the beginning is a record.
