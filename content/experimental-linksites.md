A "link in bio" page is typically a vertical list of buttons. It is a routing layer designed for maximum compliance and minimum friction.

In 2023, I built a series of experimental link-in-bio pages as freelance projects, intentionally breaking that convention. The goal was to build web interfaces that function as portfolios in their own right — non-traditional, interactive, and visually dense. 

The layout itself becomes an argument against the homogenization of social media profiles. When you refuse to use the default template, the routing page ceases to be passive infrastructure and becomes an exploratory web design exercise.
